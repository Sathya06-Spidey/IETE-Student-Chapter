Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers

The Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE) is a professional organization dedicated to the advancement of electronics, telecommunications, information technology and related engineering fields.

IETE provides students and professionals with opportunities to enhance their technical knowledge, develop innovative ideas and connect with the engineering community.

The IETE Student Chapter at SRM Chennai Ramapuram encourages students to participate in technical events, workshops, seminars, projects and other professional activities.
